Chapter Eleven, Part One. The Convergence, When Technologies Collide.


Individual technologies are transformative. Combined technologies are revolutionary. The coming decade will see artificial intelligence, robotics, biotechnology, nanotechnology, quantum computing, and other fields not merely advance in parallel, but merge-creating capabilities that no single technology could achieve alone. This chapter examines the convergences that will define the next decade: A.I.-designed nanomaterials, quantum-enhanced drug discovery, robotic surgeons guided by brain-computer interfaces, autonomous spacecraft powered by A.I., and the feedback loops that accelerate progress across all domains A.I. plus Everything, The Next Decade A.I. plus Everything.
