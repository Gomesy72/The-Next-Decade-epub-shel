(Artificial General Intelligence): A.I. systems capable of performing any intellectual task that a human can perform, across virtually all domains. Algorithm: A set of instructions for solving a problem or completing a task, often implemented in computer code. Atomically Precise Manufacturing: The ability to construct materials and devices by placing individual atoms, enabling perfect control over material properties B.C.I. (Brain-Computer Interface): A system that enables direct communication between the brain and external devices, bypassing traditional neuromuscular pathways. Blockchain: A distributed digital ledger that records transactions across many computers, making the record difficult to alter retroactively. CRISS-per: A gene-editing technology that allows precise modification of D.N.A sequences, derived from bacterial immune systems. Decoherence: The loss of quantum coherence (superposition and entanglement) due to interaction with the environment, the primary challenge in quantum computing. Deep Learning: A subset of machine learning using neural networks with many layers, enabling recognition of complex patterns. eVTOL: Electric Vertical Takeoff and Landing aircraft, designed for urban air mobility without requiring runways. Fusion: Nuclear reaction combining light atomic nuclei to form heavier ones, releasing enormous energy (the process powering stars). Gene Therapy: Treatment involving the introduction, removal, or change of genetic material within a person's cells to treat disease G.P.U. (Graphics Processing Unit): Specialized electronic circuit originally designed for graphics rendering, now widely used for A.I. computation due to parallel processing capabilities. Hallmarks of Aging: Twelve biological mechanisms that drive aging, including genomic instability, telomere attrition, epigenetic alterations, and cellular senescence. Humanoid Robot: A robot with a body shape built to resemble the human body, typically with a torso, head, two arms, and two legs. In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU): Using local materials (on the Moon, Mars, or asteroids) rather than transporting everything from Earth LIE-dar: Light Detection and Ranging, a remote sensing method using pulsed laser light to measure distances and create 3D maps L.L.M. (Large Language Model): A.I. systems trained on vast text datasets that can generate human-like text, answer questions, and perform various language tasks. Longevity Escape Velocity: The hypothetical point at which life expectancy increases by more than one year per year, potentially enabling indefinite lifespans. Machine Learning: Computer systems that improve performance on tasks through experience, without being explicitly programmed for each scenario. Molecular Nanotechnology: Engineering at the molecular scale, including the design and construction of devices at the atomic level. Nanotechnology: The manipulation of matter on an atomic, molecular, and supramolecular scale, typically 1 to 100 nanometers. Neural Network: Computing systems inspired by biological neural networks, consisting of interconnected nodes (artificial neurons) that process information. Post-Quantum Cryptography: Cryptographic algorithms designed to be secure against attacks by quantum computers. Quantum Bit (Qubit): The basic unit of quantum information, existing in superposition of 0 and 1 simultaneously until measured. Quantum Error Correction: Methods to protect quantum information from errors due to decoherence and other quantum noise. Quantum Supremacy/Advantage: The demonstration that a quantum computer can solve a specific problem faster than any classical computer. Reinforcement Learning: Machine learning where an agent learns to make decisions by performing actions and receiving rewards or penalties. Senolytics: Drugs or interventions that selectively eliminate senescent (aged, non-dividing) cells. Superintelligence: Hypothetical A.I. that surpasses human intelligence across virtually all domains, including scientific creativity, social skills, and wisdom. Transformer: A deep learning architecture using self-attention mechanisms, foundational to modern large language models. Universal Basic Income (UBI): A regular cash payment to all citizens regardless of employment status or other income. MIT: Leading A.I., robotics, and nanotechnology research.
