People have a right to modify themselves and their environment through technology, provided risks are managed responsibly and benefits are shared. Developed at the 2017 Beneficial A.I. Conference: 1. Research goal: The goal of A.I. research should be to create beneficial intelligence, not undirected intelligence. 2. Research funding: Investments in A.I. should be accompanied by funding for research ensuring beneficial use. 3. Science-policy link: There should be constructive exchange between A.I. researchers and policymakers. 4. Research culture: A culture of cooperation, trust, and transparency should be fostered. 5. Race avoidance: Teams developing A.I. systems should actively cooperate to avoid corner-cutting on safety standards. 6. Safety: A.I. systems should be safe and secure throughout their operational lifetime. 7. Failure transparency: If an A.I. system causes harm, it should be possible to ascertain why. 8. Judicial transparency: Any involvement by an autonomous system in judicial decision-making should provide adequate explanation. 9. Responsibility: Designers and builders of advanced A.I. systems are stakeholders in the moral implications of their use. 10. Value alignment: Highly autonomous A.I. systems should be designed so that their goals and behaviors can be assured to align with human values.
