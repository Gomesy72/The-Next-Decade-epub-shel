Community: Intentional building of belonging. Empathy: Technology augmenting or replacing understanding. Love: Eternal, even in changing forms By 2036, the technological infrastructure of daily life will be transformed. The homes we live in, the ways we move, the work we do, the healthcare we receive, and the entertainment we consume will all be radically different from today. Yet the fundamental human experience-love, friendship, meaning, struggle, joy-will persist. Technology changes the context but not the essence of being human. The challenge of the coming decade is not merely to develop powerful technologies but to ensure they serve human flourishing. The world of 2036 will be what we choose to make it. Technology provides possibilities; values determine outcomes. The Transformation of Science and Knowledge, The Next Decade The Transformation of Science and Knowledge AI Driven Scientific Discovery The New Scientific Method: 1 A.I. generates hypotheses from literature and data 2 A.I. designs experiments to test hypotheses 3. Robotic systems execute experiments 4 A.I. analyzes results 5 A.I. suggests next steps 6. Human scientists provide oversight, creativity, and interpretation. Domains Being Transformed: Drug discovery: A.I. designing molecules, predicting efficacy. Materials science: A.I. predicting properties of novel materials. Mathematics: A.I. generating conjectures, assisting proofs. Physics: A.I. analyzing particle collisions, predicting properties. Biology: A.I. interpreting genomic data, designing organisms. Astronomy: A.I. finding patterns in telescope data. Climate science: A.I. modeling Earth systems, predicting changes. The Scientist of 2036: Less time on data collection and analysis More time on question formulation and interpretation A.I. collaboration as standard practice Interdisciplinary by necessity Focus on what A.I. cannot do: creativity, wisdom, values The Future of Knowledge Itself The Epistemological Challenge: A.I.-generated research: How do we verify? Synthetic data: Real insights or artifacts? Overwhelming volume: How do we prioritize? Replication crisis amplified: A.I. can generate false positives rapidly Trust in knowledge systems declining. New Knowledge Institutions: A.I.-assisted peer review Continuous evaluation rather than periodic publication Open science as default Decentralized knowledge validation Prediction markets for scientific claims Adversarial collaboration between competing hypotheses. Resilience Strategies: Mindfulness: Present-moment awareness reducing anxiety about future. Stoicism: Focusing on controllable factors, accepting the rest. Community: Social support buffering individual stress. Purpose: Meaning-making providing stability amidst change. Learning: Continuous adaptation as identity rather than burden.
